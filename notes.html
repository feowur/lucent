First development distribution of lucent: native mouse/key repetition up to the current 250-CPS configuration ceiling, configurable F5 Start and Command-L Open/Close defaults, glass and custom backgrounds, and manually initiated signed Sparkle updates.

Imported backgrounds persist in the user's Application Support folder across app moves and updates; referenced legacy backgrounds are migrated without moving originals. Restart and update relaunch share lucent's existing wrap animation.

This build is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. macOS may require approval to open it and grant input access.
